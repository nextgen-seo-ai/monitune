2. Purpose of the program

MoniTune is a Windows utility that lets you control the brightness and contrast of connected external monitors, as well as the brightness of built-in laptop displays, through an icon in the notification area. External monitors are controlled over the standard DDC/CI interface, and built-in panels over the WMI interface.
